# Evolve governed Experience offline

The Harness distils Candidate Experiences from grouped successful and failed Development Rollouts, then requires leakage, generalisation, deduplication, provenance, and transfer-validation gates before Experience Promotion. Runtime may retrieve a small number of promoted Experiences by current state and Failure Pattern, but a task execution cannot rewrite global Prompt or Policy or promote its own learning; this preserves evaluation integrity and prevents one contaminated trajectory from changing later runs.

## Consequences

Profile Promotion and Experience Promotion are separate lifecycles. Retrieval outcomes provide later offline evidence, including evidence for quarantine, but never perform an online lifecycle transition.
